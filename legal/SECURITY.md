# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in lunning, please report it privately to **TODO-security@TODO-domain.com** (or open a draft security advisory on this GitHub repository).

Please include:

- A description of the issue and the impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or proof-of-concept code.
- Any relevant URLs, request payloads, or affected components.
- Whether you intend to disclose publicly, and on what timeline.

## What you can expect

- Acknowledgement within 3 business days.
- An assessment and rough timeline for a fix within 14 days.
- Public credit (if you want it) once a fix is shipped.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:

- Make a good-faith effort to avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption.
- Do not access more data than necessary to demonstrate the issue.
- Give us reasonable time to remediate before public disclosure (90 days suggested).
- Do not use the issue beyond the proof-of-concept needed to report it.

## Out of scope

- Denial-of-service attacks (don't try them).
- Social engineering of our team or users.
- Findings from automated scanners without demonstrated impact.
- Issues in third-party services (Supabase, Vercel) — report to the vendor directly.
